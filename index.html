Shahira K C, PhD | AI/ML Researcher

Hi! I’m a Post-doc at the Visual Intelligence and Learning Lab (VIGIL), IIT Hyderabad. My research focuses on AI in healthcare, vision-language models, multimodal fusion and alignment, and reducing hallucinations in AI systems. I also work on data fusion for autonomous vehicles. My recent work has been accepted at the NeurIPS Workshop (WiML) 2026. I completed my PhD from NIT Calicut, where my research focused on computer vision for assistive technology and document understanding. My doctoral work in Multimodal Document AI covered table and chart question answering, scene text recognition, document image classification, GAN-based super resolution, small text extraction from product labels, and navigation support for people with visual impairments. To be precise, my research interests are not limited to multimodal LLMs, AI for healthcare, trustworthy AI, and AI for Social Good.
